CSV files and rasters can be downloaded here after running the scripts “1_scripts/November 2026/2_Google_Earth_Engine_Scripts/extract-Sentinel2-2016”, “1_scripts/November 2026/2_Google_Earth_Engine_Scripts/extract-Sentinel2-2017”, “1_scripts/November 2026/2_Google_Earth_Engine_Scripts/extract-Sentinel2-2018”, and “1_scripts/November 2026/2_Google_Earth_Engine_Scripts/extract-Sentinel2-2019-2024” in Google Earth Engine.

Sentinel-2 data are available at 10-m resolution and include various indices obtained from red, blue, green, and infrared light reflected from Earth's surface.
